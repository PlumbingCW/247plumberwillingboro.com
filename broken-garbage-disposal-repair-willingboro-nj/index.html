How to Repair a Broken Garbage Disposal in Willingboro, NJ

Most humming or silent disposals are jammed, not dead. Here is the safe order of steps for a Willingboro, NJ kitchen, and where the do-it-yourself part stops.

By the Willingboro 24/7 Plumbing team. Updated 2026. 6 minute read.

Talk to a plumber
(609) 547-1417
Call Now

To repair a broken garbage disposal, cut power at the breaker, free the jammed flywheel with a hex key from underneath, then press the red reset button. That fixes most units that hum without spinning or sit silent. A unit that leaks, trips the breaker again or smells burnt needs a plumber.

Most disposals jam because of a hard object such as a bone fragment, a fruit pit or a bottle cap. The motor then overheats and a thermal switch trips, which is why the reset button matters.

Safety first, every time

Power off at the breaker

The wall switch is not enough. Flip the kitchen circuit off at the panel before anything else.

Never put a hand in the unit

Even with power off, the blades can cut. Use tongs or pliers to lift out anything you can see.

Work from underneath

The hex key socket sits at the bottom center of the motor housing. Use it from below, not from the drain opening.

Keep water off the motor

If you see water dripping from the body, stop and call. A leaking housing does not get better.

Freeing a jam, one step at a time

  1. 1

    Switch it off

    Turn the wall switch off, then the breaker. Confirm the unit is dead by trying the switch.

  2. 2

    Look inside with a light

    Shine a flashlight down the drain. Remove anything visible with tongs, never with fingers.

  3. 3

    Turn the hex key

    Insert the 1/4 inch hex key into the bottom socket and work it back and forth until the flywheel moves freely.

  4. 4

    Wait, then reset

    Give the motor a few minutes to cool, then press the red reset button on the bottom.

  5. 5

    Test with water

    Restore power, run cold water and flip the switch. Listen for a clean, steady spin.

Where the do-it-yourself part ends

Reasonable to try yourself

  • Freeing a jam with the hex key
  • Pressing the reset button
  • Tightening an accessible mounting nut
  • Clearing the trap below the sink

Call a plumber for

  • Leaks from the unit body
  • A motor that hums after you clear the jam
  • Repeated breaker trips
  • Corroded or cracked drain connections

A leak from the body usually means the seals have failed, and the motor is rarely worth rebuilding at that point. Prevention

Keeping the next jam from happening

Most jams trace back to a short list of items. Fibrous scraps such as celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves wrap around the shredder ring. Starchy food such as rice, pasta and potato peels swells into a paste that clings to the walls of the drain line.

  • Run cold water before, during and for 15 seconds after each use.
  • Skip bones, fruit pits, coffee grounds and eggshells in quantity.
  • Keep grease, oil and fat out of the sink, because they harden in the trap.
  • Feed scraps in slowly instead of dumping in a full load.

A unit that jams every few weeks may have a worn grinding ring or a motor losing strength. In that case, a garbage disposal repair visit or a replacement costs less than repeated trips to the reset button. We can also check the trap arm and the line behind it, since a partial clog downstream makes any disposal work harder.

What we check on a service visit

A dishwasher hose that was never knocked out during installation can cause slow drains, and we look for that too.

FAQ

Questions from Willingboro homeowners

Can I put my hand in the disposal if the power is off?

No. Use tongs or pliers, because the blades can still cut and someone may switch the power back on.

Why does my disposal hum but not spin?

The flywheel is usually jammed. Turn it free with the hex key from below, wait a few minutes, then press the reset button.

How long does a garbage disposal last?

Many last roughly 8 to 12 years, depending on use and what goes down them. A unit near the end of that range with a leak is usually better replaced.

Can I fix a leaking disposal myself?

A loose drain connection often can be tightened. A leak from the body or the lower seal means the unit is generally done.

Who do I call if the whole street's sewer is backing up in Willingboro?

Call the Willingboro Municipal Utilities Authority sewer emergency line at 609-877-4962. If only your own house is backing up, call us at (609) 547-1417 and we will check your line.

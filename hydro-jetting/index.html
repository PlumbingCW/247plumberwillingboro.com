How jetting differs from cabling

A cable machine spins a steel line through the pipe and bores a hole through the blockage. That clears a wet paper plug fast. It often leaves a coat of grease and root hair on the pipe wall, and the next clog forms on that coat.

Hydro jetting pushes a hose with a nozzle through the line and fires water backward and sideways at high pressure. The spray scours the whole wall and carries debris down to the main. For the plain single-fixture clog, a drain cleaning visit with a cable is usually the cheaper tool.

Jetting is the right tool when

  • Roots or grease keep returning
  • The line is mostly sound and round
  • A camera shows heavy buildup on the walls
  • A cable cleared it but it clogged within weeks

Jetting is the wrong tool when

  • The pipe is collapsed or has a missing section
  • A joint is offset and leaking
  • The line is thin and fragile with deep cracks
  • A single trap needs a hand auger

Why we run a camera before we jet

Water at that pressure is gentle on a healthy pipe and hard on a weak one. Levitt and Sons sold the first Willingboro homes in June 1958, so many lines in the township's older parks are now well past 65 years old. Depending on the house, a camera shows whether the pipe is cast iron, clay or plastic and whether it can take the pressure.

If the camera finds a break, we stop and quote a repair instead of washing soil into the house. That is why a sewer camera inspection often comes first, and why we do not promise jetting before we have looked.

Roots, grease and the cutting head

Roots enter at joints and cracks, then thicken into a mat that catches everything that flows past. A jetting nozzle with a cutting head, or a cable with a root cutter first, shreds the mat. Roots regrow if the opening stays, so a root-prone line may need a cleaning on a schedule or a repair at the joint.

Grease is different. It cools and hardens on the pipe wall as kitchen waste moves down the line. Jetting strips the layer that a cable leaves behind.

Kitchen lines and main lines

A kitchen line takes the heaviest grease load in the house, and it often clogs first. A main line is larger and carries everything, so a root mat there can back up the lowest drain in the house. We jet from a cleanout when one exists, so the hose goes straight in without pulling a toilet.

Willingboro Municipal Utilities Authority runs the public sewer main in the street, and its sewer emergency line is 609-877-4962. If a camera shows your lateral is clear and the water is still backing up, the main may be the cause, and a call to the Authority is the right move.

FAQ

Questions Willingboro homeowners ask

Do you handle hydro jetting emergencies at night or on weekends in Willingboro?

Yes. Our line runs 24/7 and we send a plumber for after-hours backups when a crew is free. We confirm the price before work starts and run a camera first when the line allows.

Can jetting damage an old pipe?

It can if the pipe is cracked, collapsed or very thin. That is why we look with a camera first. If the line cannot take the pressure, we clear it with a cable and quote a repair.

Is jetting better than cabling for tree roots?

Not always. A cutting head on a cable or a jetter breaks up the mat, and either can work. What matters is whether the roots keep coming back, which points to an opening in the pipe that needs a repair.

How long does the line stay clear afterward?

It depends on the cause. A scoured grease line can stay clear for a long time if you change habits. A root-prone line may need a scheduled cleaning, and we will tell you what the camera shows.

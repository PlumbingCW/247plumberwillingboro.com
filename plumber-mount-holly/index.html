Old buildings, new plumbing problems

Mount Holly is the county seat of Burlington County, with roughly 7 miles between its center and our Willingboro address, an estimate. The Mount Holly Historic District covers 260 acres and was added to the National Register of Historic Places on February 20, 1973, with 39 contributing buildings. If you need an emergency plumber in Mount Holly, NJ, our line answers 24/7.

Buildings from 1712, 1744 and 1759 are still standing here, including the Shinn Curtis Log House, the Thomas Budd House and the Brainerd Schoolhouse. None of those had indoor plumbing when they were built, so any plumbing in older structures was added later. That is practical news for an owner, because pipes run where walls allowed, not where a plan put them.

Rancocas Creek, the mill dam and where water goes

Walter Reeves built a dam on Rancocas Creek in 1677 to power a gristmill and sawmill, and the former mill land is now Mill Dam Park. Water has shaped this town for centuries. We mention it because properties near the creek can see wet ground after heavy rain, so a working sump pump or floor drain matters. We check them on any visit.

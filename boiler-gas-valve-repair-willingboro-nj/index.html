Boiler Gas Valve Repair in Willingboro, NJ: Who Fixes What

If you smell gas, leave and call the gas utility or 911 first. For the rest, here is what a plumber covers on a boiler and what belongs to another trade.

By the Willingboro 24/7 Plumbing team. Updated 2026. 6 minute read.

If you smell gas, leave the building, then call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not flip switches, use a lighter or call us first. Boiler gas valve repair after that point usually belongs to a heating specialist or the gas utility, not a general plumber.

We are plumbers, and we do not claim boiler specialist services. We handle the water side of the system and the plumbing around it. This page explains where each trade fits so you call the right one the first time.

If you smell gas

Leave at once

Get everyone, pets included, out of the building. Do not stop to open windows.

Call from outside

Use 911 or the gas utility from a neighbor's home or from the street.

Touch nothing electrical

No switches, no phones inside and no garage door openers.

Wait for clearance

Return only when the gas utility says the home is safe.

Checklist

What to look at before heating season

  • Read the pressure gauge when the boiler is cold and note the number.
  • Look for drips or stains around the relief valve, pump and valves.
  • Check that a carbon monoxide alarm sits near sleeping areas and has working batteries.
  • Listen for banging or gurgling, which can point to air or low water.
  • Keep the area around the boiler clear of boxes and chemicals.

Many residential boilers run at a modest cold pressure and rise as they heat, but the correct numbers are on the nameplate and in the manual. If the gauge reads far outside that range, stop and get it checked. A gauge that keeps falling means water is leaking out somewhere.

The plumbing side we do handle

A boiler is a closed water loop. Its relief valve opens if pressure or temperature runs too high, and it should never drip regularly. An expansion tank absorbs the water that expands as it heats, and a waterlogged tank causes pressure swings and a leaking relief valve.

We repair and replace water piping, shutoff valves, relief valves, fill valves, expansion tanks and leaking fittings. If your boiler also feeds an indirect hot water tank, our water heater repair work covers the tank and its connections. New piping runs fall under plumbing installation. Anything that touches the burner or the gas control stays with the right specialist.

If the relief valve is discharging

  1. 1

    Do not cap or plug it

    The valve protects the boiler from over-pressure. Blocking it creates a hazard.

  2. 2

    Read the gauge

    Note the pressure and temperature, and write down when the discharge happened.

  3. 3

    Turn the boiler off

    Use the service switch, then leave the system to cool before touching any pipe.

  4. 4

    Look for the cause

    A waterlogged expansion tank, a stuck fill valve or a failed thermostat can all push pressure up.

  5. 5

    Call the right trade

    Water-side faults come to us. If the burner ran away or overheated, call a heating specialist as well.

Hot water from a relief valve discharge pipe can scald, so keep children and pets away from the pipe outlet. If the pipe runs to a floor drain or the outdoors, check where the water went. Tell the plumber exactly what you saw, because it speeds up the diagnosis.

Signs that point to a plumber

  • Water around the base of the boiler
  • Relief valve dripping
  • Pressure gauge climbing past normal
  • Weeping radiator valve or pipe fitting

Signs that point to a specialist

  • Boiler will not ignite
  • Pilot or burner will not stay lit
  • Error codes on the control board
  • Clicking with no flame
Carbon monoxide matters in heating season

Any fuel-burning appliance can produce carbon monoxide if it vents poorly. A working alarm near sleeping areas is a basic safeguard. If the alarm sounds, get everyone outside and call 911, then have the system checked by a qualified specialist before it runs again.

FAQ

Questions from Willingboro homeowners

Can a plumber repair a boiler gas valve?

It depends on the work and the license held, and we do not offer boiler specialist services. We handle the plumbing side and send you to a heating specialist for burner and gas control faults.

Why does my boiler relief valve keep dripping?

A waterlogged expansion tank or high pressure is a common cause. Do not cap the valve, because it is a safety device.

Is a boiler leak always dangerous?

A water leak is damaging but not usually a gas hazard. Shut off the water feed, turn the boiler off and call a plumber.

Do I need a permit to replace boiler piping?

Permit rules vary, so ask the Willingboro building department before work starts.

Who should I call first if I smell gas at my Willingboro home?

Leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Call a plumber only after the utility has cleared the home.

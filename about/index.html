What we are, in plain words

Willingboro 24/7 Plumbing is the name homeowners in Willingboro Township call when water is in the wrong place. Our address is 4376 US Route 130, Willingboro, NJ 08046. Our phone line, (609) 547-1417, is answered 24 hours a day. When you call, we listen, tell you what to do first and schedule a licensed plumber.

We dispatch plumbers who hold the license New Jersey requires. State law, N.J.S.A. 45:14C-1, says plumbing is performed by a master plumber or an authorized plumbing contractor. We do not list a license number on this site because the plumbers sent to a job can differ. You can always ask the plumber who arrives for the license and check it with the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers.

That last step is a rule, not a slogan. Plumbing prices vary with the problem, the access and the hour, and we explain which of those applies. We do not offer a guaranteed arrival time, because traffic, storms and the job before yours can change a schedule. We tell you the plan and update you if it changes.

What we cover

Our core work is emergency repair, drains and sewers, water heaters, leaks, fixtures, pumps and repiping. The services page lists each one. For after-hours work at a business, see commercial plumbing. For hidden water problems, start with leak detection.

We also say what we do not do. Water damage drying and restoration is a separate trade, and gas leaks belong to the gas utility and 911 first. If you smell gas, leave the building before you call anyone.

Why Willingboro, and why it matters

Willingboro was built fast. Levitt and Sons sold the first homes in June 1958, and the township's population jumped from 11,861 in 1960 to 43,386 in 1970. The 2020 census counted 31,889 residents. A township built in one wave has a lot of plumbing that reached the same age at the same time, which is why we see water heater, drain and supply line calls in clusters.

The township is divided into named sections called parks, with street names starting with the same letter as the park. Buckingham Park, Pennypacker Park, Windsor Park and the rest each have their own look, and we serve all of them. Rancocas Creek forms the southwest boundary and US Route 130 forms the northwest boundary, where our office sits near the Willingboro Town Center.

Nearby towns we dispatch to

Our crews cover the neighboring townships of Delran, Burlington Township, Edgewater Park, Westampton and Mount Holly. Each has its own page with local detail. We also take calls from Moorestown, Mount Laurel, Beverly, Lumberton, Medford and Hainesport.

What we promise and what we do not

  • A clear price before work starts.
  • Same-day appointments when a crew is available.
  • A licensed plumber on the job.
  • An honest answer if the problem needs another trade or the utility.

We would rather you call the right number than the wrong one. The Willingboro Municipal Utilities Authority lists water emergencies at 609-877-3875 and sewer emergencies at 609-877-4962 on its website, and a problem in the street main is the utility's to fix. A problem inside your walls is ours.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. Read our privacy policy and terms of service for details.

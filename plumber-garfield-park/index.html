Three Garfield sections, one name

Willingboro uses the name Garfield for three neighboring sections: Garfield Park, Garfield Park East and Garfield Park North. The federal place-name database lists all three, and the Willingboro Public Library holds Levitt and Sons sales brochures for each.

That is why we cover them on one page. One map reference puts Garfield Park East about half a mile east of Garfield Park and Garfield Park North about nine tenths of a mile north-northeast of Garfield Park East. Those are rough straight-line figures, and we do not draw boundaries.

What the archive tells us about the houses

The library's finding aid lists blueprints for Garfield Park East under the style names Cape Cod, Grammercy, as the archive spells it, and Newport. A WHYY history of the town says Garfield Park was laid out so children could walk to school, and the district today lists Garfield East Early Childhood Development Center for pre-kindergarten and kindergarten.

None of that tells us which pipes are in your walls, and we do not claim it does. It does mean several house styles were built here and many have since been remodeled. We inspect before we recommend.

  • Ask which fixtures were added after the house was built.
  • Find the main water shutoff and test that it turns.
  • Locate the cleanout for the main drain if you have one.
  • Write down the water heater's age and model.

Common jobs in the Garfield sections

Most calls here are the same ones we hear across the township. Kitchen and bath drains clog, toilets run or leak at the base, and water heaters stop producing hot water. Our toilet repair page explains what we check first. For a disposal that hums or jams, see garbage disposal repair.

For a drain that keeps returning, we recommend a look with a camera so the repair targets the real cause. For a heater that is leaking from the tank, plan on replacement, because a leaking tank cannot be patched.

Utilities and permits in the Garfield sections

The Willingboro Municipal Utilities Authority lists water emergencies at 609-877-3875 and sewer emergencies at 609-877-4962 on wmua.info. A problem in the street or at the meter belongs to the utility. A problem inside your house is ours.

Permit rules vary, so ask the Willingboro building department what applies. Plumbing must be performed by a master plumber or an authorized plumbing contractor under New Jersey law.

FAQ

Questions from Garfield Park homeowners

Do you cover Garfield Park East and Garfield Park North?

Yes, we take calls from all three Garfield sections. Give us your address and we will confirm.

Why is there only one Garfield page?

The three sections share a name and sit near each other, so one page avoids repeating the same advice three times.

What should I do if a toilet is overflowing?

Close the shutoff valve behind the toilet, stop flushing and call us. Do not run drains elsewhere in the house until we have checked the line.

Can a leaking water heater tank be repaired?

Usually not. A tank that leaks from the shell is at the end of its life. A leak from a fitting can sometimes be repaired, so shut off the water and call.

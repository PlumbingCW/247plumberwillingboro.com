Open space, wet ground and sump pumps

The north portion of Rancocas State Park lies in Westampton, so a good share of the township is open ground that has to absorb heavy rain. That does not mean any one house will flood. It does mean a working sump pump matters in a storm. We test the float, the motor and the discharge path when we visit.

  • Pour water into the basin and watch the float lift and the pump start.
  • Check that the discharge pipe sends water well away from the foundation.
  • Look at the check valve for a clack when the pump stops.
  • Consider a battery backup if your power flickers in storms.

A shared ZIP and a separate township

ZIP code 08060 is shared by Westampton and Mount Holly, since the Mount Holly post office serves both. The two are separate townships with separate offices. If you are not sure where your property sits, tell us your street and we will confirm before dispatch. Our Mount Holly page covers that township.

The National Weather Service forecast office for the Philadelphia area is on Woodlane Road in Westampton, so storm warnings from that office matter locally. When a storm warning is posted, a quick sump pump test is time well spent.

What to have ready when you call from Westampton

Give us the street address and the name of the development if there is one, because Westampton addresses can be hard to find by map alone. Tell us what is happening and when it started. A pump that quit during a storm, a heater that stopped producing hot water overnight and a toilet that will not stop running each point to a different fix.

Because ZIP 08060 is shared with Mount Holly, mention the township when you call so we send the plumber to the right place. We state the price first, explain what drives it and schedule a visit for the same day when a crew is free. If the problem is in the street rather than your home, we say so and point you to the utility.

Homeowners with a finished basement should also think ahead. Know where the sump pump discharges, test the float before the rainy season and keep the pump's make and model written down. That information speeds up a repair call and helps us bring the right part on the first visit.

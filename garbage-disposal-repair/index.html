A disposal that jams, leaks or sends dishwasher water back into the sink can shut a kitchen down. We answer emergency garbage disposal repair in Willingboro, NJ calls 24/7, and same-day visits are available when a crew is free. Switch the unit off at the wall switch and the breaker, stop running water, and do not run the dishwasher until it is fixed.

Safety before anything else

  • Turn off the power at the switch and the breaker before touching anything.
  • Never put your hand into the disposal, even when it is off. Use tongs or pliers.
  • Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a jammed unit.
  • Keep children and pets out of the kitchen until the sink is dry.

Humming but not turning

A humming sound with no spinning means the flywheel is stuck. Power is off first, then insert the hex key into the socket in the center of the unit's underside and work it back and forth until it turns freely. Press the red reset button on the bottom after you free it, because overload trips it. Many units have this socket, but some models do not, so check yours.

Where the leak comes from

Top of the unit

  • Water at the sink flange
  • Failing putty or mounting ring
  • Fix: reseat the flange
  • Drips while water runs in the sink

Bottom or side

  • Water at the underside or seams
  • Cracked housing or failed seal
  • Fix: usually replacement
  • Drips even when the sink is idle

Leaks at the drain pipe connection or the dishwasher hose are often a loose clamp or a worn gasket and are quick to repair. A leak through the body of the unit cannot be patched, and we will tell you plainly when a swap is the only option.

Dishwasher backing up into the sink

The dishwasher drain hose usually connects to a side inlet on the disposal. If a new disposal was installed and the knockout plug was not removed, water has nowhere to go and backs up. A clogged inlet or a line past the unit causes the same symptom. Repair or replace

A disposal with a seized motor, a cracked body or repeated jams is usually worth replacing. A newer unit with a loose mount or a tripped overload can be repaired. Replacement includes the mount, the power connection and the dishwasher inlet. Ask the Willingboro building department whether any wiring change needs a permit, since permit rules vary, and see our plumbing installation page for related fixture work.

Replacing and installing a disposal

When a unit is past saving, we remove it, clean the sink flange and mount a new one. The job includes reconnecting the discharge pipe, the dishwasher hose and the power cord or hardwire. A heavier unit with a stainless chamber usually runs quieter and lasts longer than a bare-bones model. We can supply one or install the one you bought.

Some kitchens have no switch or outlet under the sink, and adding one is electrical work. We tell you if an electrician is needed, and the Willingboro building department can say what inspections apply. What not to grind

Grease and oil

They coat the drain and harden in cold pipes. Wipe pans into the trash.

Fibrous scraps

Celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves wrap around the blades.

Starches

Rice, pasta and potato peels swell and form a paste.

Bones, pits and shells

Hard items can jam or dull the grinding parts.

A few habits keep a unit healthy. Run cold water before, during and for 15 seconds after grinding, and feed scraps in small amounts. Cold water keeps fats solid so they move through the pipe instead of smearing on it.

FAQ

Do you handle garbage disposal repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Willingboro?

Yes. Our line is open 24/7 and we send a licensed plumber when a crew is free. The price is confirmed before work starts.

Is it safe to use a hex key on my disposal?

Yes, if the power is off at the switch and the breaker. Never put your hand into the unit, even when it appears dead.

Why does my dishwasher drain into the sink?

The drain hose may be blocked, the disposal inlet may still have its plug, or the line past the unit may be clogged. We check all three.

Can you install a new disposal?

Yes. We mount it, connect power and the dishwasher inlet, and test for leaks.

Local knowledge

Plumbing in a Levitt-built township

Willingboro was Levittown, New Jersey for a few years. Levitt and Sons sold the first homes in June 1958, residents voted to rename the town Levittown Township in November 1959, and a 1963 referendum restored the Willingboro name. The township counted 11,442 housing units in the 2010 census, so a large share of homes are the same age and have been through the same plumbing cycles.

Many homes sit in named sections called parks, such as Buckingham Park, Pennypacker Park, Windsor Park, Garfield Park, Rittenhouse Park, Twin Hill, Hawthorne Park, Fairmount Park, Deer Park, Somerset and Millbrook. Country Club Ridge was built around a golf course designed by Robert Trent Jones. A plumber who knows these sections knows what a neighbor's repair can tell you, and we always check your own pipes before we quote.

Rancocas Creek forms the southwest boundary and US Route 130 forms the northwest boundary. Our address is on US Route 130 near the Willingboro Town Center, which is anchored by the Willingboro Public Library. The Willingboro Municipal Utilities Authority handles water and sewer, and it lists separate emergency lines for each. We help you decide which call to make first.

The township's January average is about 33 degrees Fahrenheit, so frozen pipes and tired water heaters are winter regulars. Read the utility's annual water quality report at wmua.info, test your tap, and call us if you spot staining, low pressure or noise from the heater.

Is your emergency plumber line really open 24/7?

Which areas do you cover from Willingboro?

What should I do before the plumber arrives?

Shut the main water valve for a leak, stop using drains for a backup, switch off power near standing water, and keep children and pets away. Our dispatcher will tell you what applies to your problem.

How do I know a plumber is licensed in New Jersey?

State law, N.J.S.A. 45:14C-1, says plumbing is performed by a master plumber or an authorized plumbing contractor. Ask for the license, and check it with the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers.

Do you handle commercial plumbing emergencies?

Yes. We take after-hours calls from businesses and landlords and quote commercial jobs per job. See our commercial plumbing page.

A section planned around a golf course

Country Club Ridge was laid out in the 1960s around a golf course designed by Robert Trent Jones, according to the township's history on Wikipedia. The Willingboro Public Library holds a Levitt and Sons sales brochure for Country Club Ridge, which confirms that Levitt marketed it as its own section.

The township's Country Club pool and administration building are at 440 Beverly-Rancocas Road, and Lizzie Mae Morris Memorial Field is on Beverly-Rancocas Road as well. We do not claim those sit inside the section. We mention them as nearby landmarks that help people place the area.

Why we focus on diagnosis here

A section laid out in the 1960s is still decades old, and homes in it have had time to be remodeled. We make no claim about the age or type of pipes in any particular house. Instead we use tools that show the real condition. A camera on a sewer line shows cracks, roots or sags. A leak survey finds water you cannot see.

That approach keeps repairs small. If a camera shows a clear line, you avoid digging. If it shows a failure, you know exactly where to repair.

When a cable is enough

  • A single sink or tub that is slow
  • A clog that has not returned
  • Water draining normally elsewhere

When to look inside the line

  • Several fixtures back up together
  • A clog that keeps returning
  • A gurgle in a drain after flushing

Services we use most near the golf course

Water heater trouble, drain clogs and unexplained wet spots are the usual reasons for a call. See water heater repair if the pilot will not stay lit or the water is lukewarm. For a line that backs up repeatedly, sewer line cleaning clears the main, and a camera inspection explains why it happens.

If your basement or crawl space shows water after heavy rain, we also install and repair sump pumps. Whether a particular house has one is for you or an inspector to confirm.

Utilities, permits and licensing

The Willingboro Municipal Utilities Authority lists water emergencies at 609-877-3875 and sewer emergencies at 609-877-4962 on wmua.info. A problem in the street or at the meter belongs to the utility. A problem inside your house is ours.

Permit rules vary, so ask the Willingboro building department what applies. Ask any plumber for a master plumber license and check it with the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers.

FAQ

Questions from Country Club Ridge homeowners

Where is Country Club Ridge?

It is a named residential section of Willingboro, laid out in the 1960s around a golf course. We confirm your address when you call because boundaries are not sharply drawn.

Is the pool in Country Club Ridge?

The township's Country Club pool is at 440 Beverly-Rancocas Road. We cannot say whether a given street is inside the section, so call the township for pool questions.

Can you inspect a sewer line with a camera?

Yes. A camera inspection shows the condition of the line and guides any repair. Ask about it when a clog keeps returning.

Do you repair sump pumps?

Yes, we repair and install them. See our sump pump page. We do not assume a home has one, so we check first.

Where Buckingham Park sits in Willingboro

Buckingham Park is one of the named sections, called parks, that Levitt and Sons laid out when it built Willingboro as its third and largest Levittown. The Willingboro Public Library's local history collection files a folder called Homes in Oldest 3 Parks under Somerset, Buckingham and Pennypacker. That places Buckingham Park among the first sections built.

The federal place-name database lists it as a populated place in Willingboro Township. One map reference puts Somerset Park about half a mile north, Pennypacker Park about eight tenths of a mile southwest and Hawthorne Park about nine tenths of a mile east. Those are straight-line estimates. We do not claim exact boundaries. A handy clue is that each park's street names start with the park's first letter, so look for streets that begin with B.

What an early section means for plumbing

An early section has had the most years of owners, repairs and remodeling. It does not tell us what is inside your walls. Levitt designed its houses to be expanded, according to a WHYY history of the town, and owners began changing them quickly. That means a bath, kitchen or laundry may have been moved or added at some point, and the plumbing may not match the original plan.

So we look before we assume. A camera shows the real condition of a drain line, and a leak survey finds water you cannot see. We do not guess at pipe materials from the age of a house.

Utility and permit questions

The Willingboro Municipal Utilities Authority lists water emergencies at 609-877-3875 and sewer emergencies at 609-877-4962 on wmua.info. A problem in the street or at the meter belongs to the utility. A problem inside your house is ours.

The utility also keeps a Lead Service Line Inventory and asks residents to help identify service line materials. If you are unsure what runs from the street to your house, say so when you call. We can explain what we can and cannot tell from inside the house.

Permit rules vary, so ask the Willingboro building department what applies to your job. Plumbing in New Jersey must be performed by a master plumber or an authorized plumbing contractor, and you can ask to see the license.

FAQ

Questions from Buckingham Park homeowners

Do you serve all of Buckingham Park?

We take calls from homes in and around Buckingham Park. Boundaries between the parks are not always obvious, so give us your street address and we will confirm.

How do I know if my street is in Buckingham Park?

Streets in each park start with the park's first letter, with a few exceptions. Your property records or the township can confirm the section.

Who do I call for a problem in the street?

Call the utility for anything in the street or at the meter. WMUA lists water emergencies at 609-877-3875 and sewer emergencies at 609-877-4962.

Is an older section more likely to have plumbing trouble?

Age alone does not tell us that. Fixtures wear out and remodels change things, so we inspect the actual house instead of assuming.

Buckingham Park sits next to other Willingboro sections. Pennypacker Park is another of the first three, and the full list of places we serve is on the service areas page. Return to the Willingboro home page for everything we do.

About Millbrook Park

Millbrook Park is a named residential section of Willingboro. The Willingboro Public Library's local history collection lists a Millbrook Park Civic Association among the township's clubs and organizations, which shows the area has long been recognized by its own name. One map reference puts Millbrook Park about a mile south of Buckingham Park and about 1.1 miles west-northwest of Garfield Park East. Those are rough straight-line estimates.

Do not confuse it with Millcreek Park. The township lists that park separately, at 300 Beverly-Rancocas Road and Pagent Lane.

Hiring a plumber when neighbors compare notes

Neighbors in an organized area often share names of plumbers. That is useful, but a recommendation does not replace a few checks. New Jersey requires plumbing to be performed by a master plumber or an authorized plumbing contractor. Ask to see the license and check it with the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers.

Ask for the price before work begins and what is included. Ask whether a permit is needed. Permit rules vary, so ask the Willingboro building department what applies to your job.

Jobs we handle for Millbrook Park homes

Typical calls include drain cleaning, water heater repair and faucet repair. We also take new fixture work. See plumbing installation when you are adding or replacing a sink, toilet or line.

If your water bill rises with no change in use, a hidden leak may be the cause. The utility runs a Smart Water Meter Portal listed on wmua.info. If you have access, a usage line that never drops to zero overnight is worth mentioning when you call.

Utility numbers

The Willingboro Municipal Utilities Authority lists water emergencies at 609-877-3875 and sewer emergencies at 609-877-4962 on wmua.info. A problem in the street or at the meter belongs to the utility. A problem inside your house is ours.

FAQ

Questions from Millbrook Park homeowners

Does Millbrook Park have its own association?

The library's local history collection lists a Millbrook Park Civic Association. We do not know its current status, so check with the township or your neighbors.

How do I verify a plumber's license?

Ask for the master plumber license and check it with the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers. State law says plumbing is performed by a master plumber or an authorized plumbing contractor.

Is Millbrook Park the same as Millcreek Park?

No. Millcreek Park is a township park at 300 Beverly-Rancocas Road and Pagent Lane. Millbrook Park is a residential section.

Will I know the price before the work begins?

Yes. We state the price before work starts and explain what changes it, such as access, parts and time of day.

The water service line is the pipe that carries water from the street or meter to your house. When it breaks, you may see a soaked lawn, a sudden drop in pressure or no water at all. We answer 24/7 for emergency water service line repair in Willingboro, NJ, and book same-day visits when a crew is free. While you wait, close the main valve inside the house and keep people away from any sinkhole or soft ground.

Signs the line is leaking

Meter keeps moving with everything off

Close every tap and appliance, then watch the meter. A moving dial points to a leak on your side of it.

Soft or wet patch in the yard

Water coming up between the street and the house, especially in dry weather, is a strong clue.

Low pressure everywhere

A leak or a corroded line can cut flow to the whole house at once.

Brown or cloudy water

Discoloration can come from a damaged line or from disturbed sediment, so tell us when it began.

Where utility responsibility ends

Willingboro water is supplied by the Willingboro Municipal Utilities Authority, and the dividing point between its pipe and yours is not the same at every property. Do not assume. Call WMUA at 609-877-2900, or 609-877-3875 for a water emergency, and ask where its responsibility stops at your address. Then tell us what they said and we will repair the part that belongs to you.

Lead service line inventory and your pipe material

WMUA publishes a Lead Service Line Inventory page asking residents to help identify the material of their service lines. That matters because a line that is being repaired or replaced is the best time to learn what it is made of. Tell us if you have seen the pipe entering the house, and we can look at it and describe what we see. We do not test water for lead, so use the WMUA water quality report and a certified lab for that.

A leak detection visit can pinpoint the break before we dig, which keeps the opening small.

Trenchless versus open trench

A trench is the straightforward method. We dig down to the pipe, replace the bad section or the whole line, and backfill. Trenchless methods pull new pipe through the old path with fewer holes, which helps if the line runs under a driveway, patio or mature landscaping. Not every line suits it, and we tell you plainly which approach fits. Restoring lawn, concrete or asphalt is done by another trade.

Low water pressure

Low pressure does not always mean a broken line. A partly closed main valve, a failing pressure regulator, clogged fixtures or a corroded line can all cause it. We check the valve and regulator first, then compare pressure at the hose bib and inside. If the number drops between the two, the line is a suspect.

What happens on a service line visit

  1. 1

    Confirm the leak

    We check the meter, the main valve and the pressure at an outside hose bib to see whether the loss is in the line or inside the house.

  2. 2

    Locate the line

    We trace where the pipe enters the house and where it runs toward the street, using access points and a locator when needed.

  3. 3

    Explain the options

    You get the repair and replacement choices, including the cost of each, before any digging begins.

  4. 4

    Repair and test

    After the fix we restore flow, check pressure and look for any remaining wet spots.

If the water is already off at the street because of a utility shutoff, tell us when you call so the plumber arrives with the right parts. A line that has been dry for a while may also need flushing before fixtures are used again, so run cold taps for a few minutes once service returns.

Permits and digging

Permit rules vary, so ask the Willingboro building department at 609-877-2200 before any excavation. Utility mark-outs are also needed before digging, and we handle that call as part of scheduling.

FAQ

Questions Willingboro homeowners ask

Do you handle water service line emergencies at night or on weekends in Willingboro?

Yes. Our line runs 24/7 and we offer after-hours visits subject to crew availability, with a clear price before work starts.

Is the service line my responsibility or the utility's?

It depends on the address and where the meter and curb stop sit. Call WMUA at 609-877-2900 to confirm, then call us for the part that is yours.

How can I tell if the meter shows a leak?

Turn off every fixture and appliance, then watch the dial. If it keeps moving, there is a leak somewhere past the meter.

Can you replace a lead service line?

We can replace the private side of a water service line with modern pipe once we confirm who owns which part. Check the WMUA inventory page and call them first.

We repair service lines for homes in Edgewater Park and Westampton as well. Tell us the street when you call.

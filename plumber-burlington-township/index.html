Reaching Burlington Township from US Route 130 and I-295

Burlington Township lies north of Willingboro and is served by US Route 130, Interstate 295 and County Route 541, which is why an emergency plumber in Burlington Township, NJ is a short run from our base. Roughly 6 miles separate the two, an estimate that varies with traffic. We answer 24/7 and send crews same-day when one is free.

The township is the largest of our five nearby pages, at 14.02 square miles. It is home to the Marketplace at Burlington, the former Burlington Center Mall that closed in 2018, and to the headquarters of Burlington Coat Factory. Large buildings and big properties have their own plumbing needs, so we cover both homes and businesses here.

A township that grew fast

Population here rose from 3,441 in 1950 to 10,621 in 1970, and the township counted 23,983 residents in 2020. That growth means many homes date from the mid-20th century. Plumbing installed in those decades is now at the age where water heaters, supply lines and drain lines get replaced, so repair calls and replacement quotes are common.

What to have ready when you call from Burlington Township

Give the dispatcher your street and the nearest cross street, since the township is large and several roads share similar names. Say what you see: water on the floor, a drain that will not clear, a heater that is leaking or a smell. If you can safely reach the main water valve, tell us whether it turns, because that changes the first instructions we give you.

Business owners should say whether the problem is inside a tenant space or in shared plumbing. That tells us who to contact and whether the building manager needs to be on the line.

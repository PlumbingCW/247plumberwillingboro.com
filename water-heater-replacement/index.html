A tank that leaks from its shell cannot be repaired, and every hour it runs can add water damage. This is our emergency water heater replacement page. We answer 24/7 and schedule same-day replacements in Willingboro when a crew and a matching unit are available.

Shut the cold water valve above the heater, turn off its power or gas control, and put a bucket or wet vac under any drip. Move boxes and electronics off the floor nearby. Then call us and we will plan the swap.

Replace now or keep repairing

Age, leaks and repair history decide it. A unit past about 10 years with a rusted base, repeated element failures or a cracked tank is cheaper to replace. A newer unit with one bad part belongs on our repair page instead.

Repair or replace at a glance
SituationUsual answerWhy
Leak from the tank body.Replace.The steel shell has failed and cannot be patched.
Failed part on a unit under 8 years old.Repair.The rest of the tank is likely sound.
Unit past 12 years with sediment noise.Replace.Efficiency is low and failure risk is rising.
Household has grown or demand changed.Replace with a larger or tankless unit.Capacity should match the home.

Choosing the right unit

We match the replacement to your fuel, venting and household size. Gas tank units need a vent that draws properly. Electric units need the right breaker and wire size. If you want endless hot water, we can quote a tankless conversion, though that adds gas line, venting and sometimes electrical work.

  • Tank size that fits the number of bathrooms and people at home.
  • Venting type and condition for gas units.
  • New shutoff valves, an expansion tank where required and a proper drain pan.
  • Removal and disposal of the old tank.

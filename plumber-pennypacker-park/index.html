Pennypacker Park and its landmarks

Pennypacker Park is another named section from the original Levitt plan. The Willingboro Public Library files it in the same Homes in Oldest 3 Parks folder as Somerset and Buckingham. Streets there start with the letter P, which is the township's naming pattern for each park.

Two local landmarks help people find the area. The township lists Pennypacker Pool at 13 Pinetree Lane and Pennypacker Field at Pennypacker Drive and Sunset. Each park once had its own pool. By the 1990s only Pennypacker Park and Country Club Park still ran summer pools, now called the community pools.

A seasonal plumbing calendar for the area

Willingboro's January average is about 33 degrees Fahrenheit, so cold snaps matter here. Plan around the calendar instead of waiting for a failure.

In late fall, disconnect garden hoses and close any interior valve that feeds an outdoor spigot. In winter, know where your main shutoff is, and tell us if a pipe along an exterior wall has frozen before.

  1. 1

    Fall

    Disconnect hoses and check outdoor faucets. See faucet repair for spigots that drip.

  2. 2

    Winter

    Keep a main valve key handy and read our burst pipe repair steps.

  3. 3

    Spring

    Turn outdoor water back on slowly and check for drips before a hose goes on.

  4. 4

    Summer

    Higher water use makes a running toilet or slow drain easier to notice. See toilet repair.

Everyday jobs we handle for Pennypacker Park homes

The work that fills most calls is ordinary: clogged kitchen and bath drains, tired water heaters, dripping faucets and running toilets. For a clog we use cables and, for stubborn lines, hydro jetting. For a heater that no longer keeps up, we explain repair against replacement and give you the price first.

If water is appearing where it should not, we start with leak detection. That avoids cutting into a wall until we know where the water is coming from.

Utility numbers and permits

The Willingboro Municipal Utilities Authority lists water emergencies at 609-877-3875 and sewer emergencies at 609-877-4962 on wmua.info. A problem in the street or at the meter belongs to the utility. A problem inside your house is ours.

Permit rules vary, so ask the Willingboro building department what applies to your project. Ask any plumber for a master plumber license, as state law requires.

FAQ

Questions from Pennypacker Park homeowners

Where is Pennypacker Park?

It is one of the township's named sections. Streets there start with P, and the township lists Pennypacker Pool at 13 Pinetree Lane and Pennypacker Field at Pennypacker Drive and Sunset.

Do you work near the community pool?

We work at homes and businesses in the area. The pool is operated by the township, so call the township for pool questions.

When should I shut off outdoor water?

Before freezing weather arrives. If your outdoor faucet has an interior shutoff, close it, drain the line and remove the hose. If you are unsure, ask us.

Can you tell me what my pipes are made of?

Not from the street. A visit and, if needed, a camera show what is there. We do not guess from the age of a house.

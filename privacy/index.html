Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you call us or browse this website.

Talk to a plumber
(609) 547-1417
Call Now

Calls

Our service is by phone. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your name, phone number, address and a description of the problem so we can schedule a plumber and quote a price.

What we do with call information

We use it to answer your call, schedule work, give you a price and follow up on a job. We share it with the plumbers assigned to your job and with service providers who help run our phone line. We do not sell your personal information.

This website

This website has no forms and does not ask you to enter personal information. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and page requests, to keep the site secure and working. Embedded Google Maps may set cookies or collect data under Google's own policies when they load.

Keeping information safe

We limit access to call information to people who need it for a job. No method of storage is perfect, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to any records we must keep. Contact us at contact@247plumberwillingboro.com or call (609) 547-1417.

Changes and contact

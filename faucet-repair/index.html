A dripping faucet wastes water and stains the sink, and a handle that will not turn is just as annoying. This page covers faucet repair in Willingboro, NJ for kitchens, baths, showers and outdoor spigots. We book visits through our 24/7 line, and same-day appointments are available when a crew is free.

One case is different. If a valve will not shut off and water is spraying or running freely, treat it as urgent. Close the small angle stop under the sink by turning it clockwise, or shut the main water valve where the line enters the house, and then call us.

Which type of faucet you have

Most faucets fall into four types, and the type decides the repair. Knowing yours helps us bring the right parts the first time.

Faucet types and typical repairs
TypeHow it feelsUsual repair
Compression.Two handles that you tighten to stop the flow.New washers and seats.
Cartridge.One or two handles that move smoothly.A new cartridge.
Ball.A single handle over a rounded cap.New seals, springs or ball.
Ceramic disc.A single lever that feels very smooth.A new disc cylinder.

Kitchen and bathroom sink problems

Drip from the spout

A worn washer, seat or cartridge lets water past when the handle is off.

Leak at the handle

A worn O-ring or packing nut usually lets water escape around the stem.

Weak flow

Mineral buildup in the aerator is common, and cleaning it often helps.

Water under the sink

A loose supply line, a bad angle stop or a cracked drain can all cause it.

Willingboro Municipal Utilities Authority publishes an annual water quality report at wmua.info. Read it and test your tap if you see white crust on fixtures, because mineral buildup shortens the life of cartridges and aerators. Monthly hydrant flushing can also stir sediment, so a brief burst of cloudy water is worth checking against the utility notice.

Shower valves and tub spouts

A shower that drips after you turn it off often has a worn cartridge in the valve behind the wall. A tub spout that sends water to the showerhead even when the diverter is down may have a failed diverter. Some valves are reached from the front trim, and some need an access panel on the other side of the wall.

Hose bibs and outdoor spigots

An outdoor spigot that drips at the stem or leaks inside the wall needs attention before winter. January averages near 33 degrees Fahrenheit in Willingboro, so disconnect hoses in the fall and shut off the indoor valve to a hose bib if you have one. A frozen spigot can crack and leak inside the wall once the thaw comes.

Replacing a cartridge is usually a short job, but the right part matters. Faucets from different makers look alike and fit differently, so a photo of the handle and the label helps. If the model is unknown, we remove the old cartridge and match it by hand.

Burst or broken supply valves

The valve under the sink is a small part with a big job. If it cracks, drips from the stem or will not close, we replace it and the supply line at the same time. That gives you a working shutoff for next time, and it is a better fix than tightening a worn one.

Repair or replace

Repair when

  • The faucet is a recent model
  • Parts are available
  • Only one component has failed
  • The finish still looks good

Replace when

  • The body is corroded or cracked
  • Parts are discontinued
  • The faucet has leaked more than once
  • You want a new style or feature

If you decide to swap, see our plumbing installation page, and bring us a faucet you already bought or ask us to supply one. Ask the Willingboro building department whether a larger fixture change needs a permit.

Water that appears under a sink is not always the faucet. We dry the area, run each fixture in turn and watch the supply lines, the drain and the shutoff valves until we see where the drip starts. The Smart Water Meter Portal from the Willingboro Municipal Utilities Authority can also show whether water keeps flowing when everything in the house is off.

FAQ

Questions Willingboro homeowners ask

My faucet will not shut off at all. What do I do?

Close the angle stop under the sink by turning it clockwise. If it will not move, close the main water valve and call us, because a valve that will not shut off is an urgent case.

Can you fix a faucet that drips only when hot?

Often yes. One side of a mixing valve or cartridge has worn out, and we can usually replace that part.

Is it cheaper to repair or to replace a faucet?

For a recent model with one failed part, repair is usually cheaper. A corroded faucet or one that has leaked before is often better replaced.

Do you fix outdoor spigots?

Yes. We repair or replace hose bibs, and we suggest shutting off the indoor valve before winter.

Do you replace the valve under the sink?

Yes. We replace the angle stop and the supply line together so you have a working shutoff.

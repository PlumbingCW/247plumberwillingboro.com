Delran sewer bills and what a leak can do to them

Delran's Sewer Department bills sewer service quarterly, with due dates on April 1, July 1, October 1 and January 1. Its bills are based on water use in January, February and March of the prior year, according to the township's own page. A leak running through that winter quarter can raise a bill for a full year, so a running toilet or a hidden pipe leak is worth fixing early.

The township page does not say who supplies water, so check your water bill for the utility's name and number. If the sewer service in question is the township's, the Sewer Department office in the Township Building at 900 Chester Avenue handles billing questions at 856-461-2284.

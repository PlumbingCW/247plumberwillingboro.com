Call us, day or night

Phone is how we work, because plumbing problems need a person who can ask follow-up questions. Call (609) 547-1417 any time. We answer 24 hours a day, every day, and schedule same-day visits when a crew is free.

Email is for non-urgent questions: contact@247plumberwillingboro.com. For anything involving water, gas or sewage in your home, please call.

What to tell the dispatcher

  • Your street address and town.
  • What is happening: leak, backup, no hot water or something else.
  • Whether you have shut off the water and where the main valve is.
  • If anyone is at risk from electricity or gas.
  • A callback number in case we get disconnected.

Where we are

Willingboro 24/7 Plumbing, 4376 US Route 130, Willingboro, NJ 08046. This is our business address and dispatch base. Most work happens at your property, not ours, so please call before you drive anywhere. We serve Willingboro and nearby towns such as Delran and Burlington Township.

Other numbers worth having

The Willingboro Municipal Utilities Authority lists water emergencies at 609-877-3875 and sewer emergencies at 609-877-4962 on wmua.info. The Willingboro building department's Construction Official is at 609-877-2200, extension 1016, according to the township website. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

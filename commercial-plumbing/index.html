Drain, grease line and floor sink problems

Commercial drains carry more waste than residential ones, and kitchens add fat that coats the pipe wall. We clear lines with cable machines and follow with hydro jetting when grease has narrowed the pipe. Floor sinks and floor drains are cleaned at the same visit.

If a line backs up again within weeks of a cleaning, a camera run shows whether grease, a bellied section or a broken joint is the cause. The WMUA sewer emergency line is 609-877-4962, which matters when the blockage turns out to sit in the public main rather than in your lateral.

Grease trap cleaning

A grease trap or interceptor only protects the sewer if it is cleaned before it fills. We can pump or scoop and clean a trap, check the baffles and flow control, and tell you if the unit looks undersized for the kitchen. How often it needs service depends on volume, so we do not promise a fixed schedule. Check your lease and any local rules for required cleaning logs.

Backflow testing

Some commercial buildings have backflow preventers that must be tested on a schedule. Annual testing requirements vary by device and use, so ask the water utility or the Willingboro building department which tests apply to your property. We can help you understand what you have, and we will tell you plainly if a certified test falls outside what we do.

Leaks, storm drains and exposed lines

A hidden leak in a ceiling or a slab shows up as a high water bill or a stain, and our leak detection work finds the source before anyone opens a wall. Roof drains and outdoor storm drains can clog with leaves and litter, and we can clear the ones on your property. January averages near 33 degrees Fahrenheit here, so exposed lines in unheated storage areas deserve insulation before the cold sets in.

Tenant or landlord: who pays

The lease decides, not the plumber. As a general pattern, the landlord handles building systems such as the main line and shared risers, and the tenant handles fixtures inside the leased space. Leases differ, so read yours before approving work. We write a clear invoice that names the location of the repair, which makes it easier to settle between the two parties.

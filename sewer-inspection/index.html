A sewer camera inspection in Willingboro, NJ puts a waterproof camera inside your main line and records what it sees. Our booking line runs 24/7 and same-day appointments are available when a crew is free. It is the quickest way to learn whether you have a clog, a crack, a root mat or a pipe that has settled.

If the line is backing up now, treat it as an emergency and call our urgent line, because a full blockage must be cleared before the camera can pass.

What the camera shows

Cracks and breaks

Fractures show as dark lines or gaps in the pipe wall.

Roots

Fine hair or thick mats enter at joints and crowd the bore.

Bellies

Standing water along a run marks a low spot.

Offsets

Misaligned joints show as a step or a gap.

Scale and buildup

Rough walls show where grease or corrosion has narrowed the pipe.

Push camera and locator

A push camera is fed down a cleanout or through a pulled toilet on a flexible rod. A transmitter in the head sends a signal to a hand-held locator above ground. The locator marks the depth and position of any fault, so a repair crew knows where to dig and a homeowner knows how far from the house the problem sits.

We record the run so you can see the footage and keep a copy. A recording is useful for comparing before and after a repair and for sharing with a contractor or an insurer.

When to book one

  • After repeat clogs that a cable clears only for a short time.
  • Before buying an older house, so a failing line is a known cost and not a surprise.
  • Before selling, if you want to answer a buyer's question with footage.
  • Before landscaping or building over the line's path.
  • After a backup, to learn what caused it.

For a home purchase, the camera is general advice and not a legal inspection. Ask your real estate attorney or agent how a report fits your contract. Levitt and Sons sold the first Willingboro homes in June 1958, so a line in an older park may be well past 65 years old, and a camera shows its actual condition.

After repeat clogs

A line that clogs two or three times in a year is telling you something. A cable clears the symptom, while a camera shows the cause. We often recommend the camera after the second clog, before money goes into a fix that may not hold.

If the footage shows a clean pipe, you know the clogs came from habits such as grease or wipes. If it shows roots or a belly, you have a clear case for a repair, and you can compare it with scheduled main line cleaning on cost.

Limits of a camera inspection

The camera cannot see through a fully blocked pipe, so we may clear the line first, which is a separate step. It shows the inside only, and cannot say what is happening in the soil. Each lateral is its own pipe, even on the same street in a park such as Pennypacker, Millbrook or Deer Park, so one house's footage does not describe a neighbor's.

We report what we see and do not guess. If the footage is unclear, we say so, and we may suggest a second pass after cleaning.

FAQ

Questions Willingboro homeowners ask

How long does a sewer camera inspection take?

A typical run takes a short visit, depending on line length and whether it needs cleaning first. We tell you what we expect when we book.

Can you inspect a line that is fully blocked?

Not until it is cleared. The camera cannot pass a solid blockage, so we may cable the line first. That step is quoted before we start.

Will the camera damage my pipe?

No. The head is small, flexible and smooth, and it rides along the pipe floor. We do not force it past a hard stop. If it will not pass, we tell you why and stop.

Do I get a copy of the video?

Yes. We record the run and give you a copy so you can share it with a contractor or keep it for your records.

Should I get one before buying an older house?

It is general advice, not a legal requirement. A camera can show whether the line is sound, and it may help you plan for repairs. Talk to your attorney about how it fits your contract.

If the Authority's main is the problem, what then?

The Willingboro Municipal Utilities Authority handles the main, and its sewer emergency line is 609-877-4962. We can show you where our footage ends and the main begins.
